Undergraduate Computer Science courses

A degree in computer science at a UK university will provide you with the key skills you need to form companies and organizations. New tools, programs and development kits can help improve your knowledge and skills in areas such as IT, production of games, software engineering, design, and health. Computer Science is at the forefront of UK universities, with several departments doing work for the world's biggest corporations.

Computer Science modules of study include database architecture, computer hardware, network infrastructure, and the internet, as well as cutting-edge technologies such as artificial intelligence, virtual reality, and application design. The United Kingdom has played an important role in software growth and continues to produce innovative developments in the 21st century.

Career and Salary

Computer science positions in every sector are strongly in demand. In addition, the high status of computer science schools in the UK has resulted in increased support for these divisions in the field of computer science. This rise in funding translates into a number of consequences in the field of computer science for foreign students, including notable diversification and career specialization. The area of computer science provides many possible uses for the degrees being earned by foreign students.

Computer Science graduates boast desirable employment opportunities as software developers, system analysts, programmers, independent consultants, data analysts and managers. After graduation, initial employment will involve training outlines, entry-level programmer positions and junior members of project teams. The need for talent across all sectors and industries allows Computer Science graduates to dictate which field they wish to work in.

Their average salary can start from £25,000 for professionals and £16,500 for non-professional.

Fees Structure

Based on the educational institute and course, fees may change depending on different factors. Generally, the tuition fees for a full-time international degree is £21,300 annually.

All entry fees are subjected to be reviewed annually and can change without any prior notice (fees are typically set for international students, for the entry period).

International applicants will be required to pay a £1,000 additionally as a deposit on their tuition fees before an acceptance certificate for studies is granted. This fee will only be refunded if an immigrate visa is returned back.

Entry Requirement

Most courses do not define prerequisite subjects that are compulsory to study in school, but certain schools demand high achievement in their school. In certain schools, the prerequisite subjects may include mathematics and science.

Typical International Baccalaureate requirements: Minimum 34 points
Typical A-levels requirements: ABB with outstanding grades in mathematics and computing
International students may require to take the accredited testing system to demonstrate English proficiency.

•    For IELTS: 6.5 bands overall, with no lower than 5.5 bands in every module.
•    For TOEFL with an overall point of 100, including 25 in the written section
